About

MathLLM is a small educational app for solving and generating mathematical problems using local and cloud-based language models. The goal is to make math support more accessible by combining image-based input, reasoning, and clear explanations in one place.

MathLLM allows the users to use what are known as Large Language Models, which are AI models that can understand and generate human-like text. These models can be used to solve math problems, generate new problems, and provide explanations for solutions. Once you go back to the homepage click on the LLMs to Use button to learn more about how the app uses LLMs and which models are supported.
